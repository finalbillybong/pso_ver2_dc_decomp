## Method

Batch 251 comes from hand-finished near misses among the reviewed candidate units (batch 243). The draft generator did not change. Two separate scratch passes started from generated drafts that differed from the reference in 0–20 lines, and edited each one until its whole range compiled exactly. Both passes followed the rules of batches 241–243:
- at most ten distinct hypotheses per target, counting earlier probes, with identical compiler outputs counted once;
- ordinary C or C++ only, with the pinned compiler and flags;
- full-range exact only;
- no declaration-order search.

The two passes found 260 exact sources. For 147 of them the generator, with the listing's delay-slot fix and the rules disclosed in batch 250, now produces an exact draft by itself; those are admitted in batch 250 as unedited generated drafts. This batch admits the other 113.

Between them the passes compiled 520 and 245 hypotheses. The most on one target was 8, counting earlier probes. One labelled declaration-order comparison was made: it showed that the placement of a new local does not decide `0x8c021e84`. For other admitted sources that add a local, another placement of its declaration was also exact.

Held back and not trialled: `0x8c0b47d8`, whose near-exact source needs a fourth argument that the admitted callee does not take.

The history guard refused no attempt. Previously blocked targets stay blocked, and the three drafts held back in batch 224 remain held back.

Before admission, the boundaries of every unit were checked:
- each entry follows a literal pool (106) or a function end (7);
- no unit overlaps an admitted function or another unit;
- no branch from outside a unit lands in it;
- every entry is 4-aligned.

## Observed techniques

- Constructors, factories and destructors whose epilogue moves the object or the allocated block into `r0` return it. Drafts written as `void` miss the move.
- An empty then-arm, or a store followed by `return`, reproduces an unfilled `bra` delay slot.
- A float-to-unsigned fixup sequence with a 2^31 or 2^32 correction comes from a plain cast.
