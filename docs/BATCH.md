## Method

Batch 192 used a new private draft generator, `draft.py`, kept in the research workspace.
- It walks a candidate's reviewed listing and literal pool symbolically.
- It infers struct layouts from the offsets accessed.
- It emits a first-draft C or C++ source that applies cookbook idioms CW01, CW10, CW14, CW16, CW21 and CW22.
- Regions it cannot structure, such as switches and multi-exit loops, are emitted as TODO comments.

A companion evaluator compiled each draft twice, once with data globals as address casts and once as extern symbols. It compared each build against the reference in scratch.

The evaluator was first calibrated on already-admitted batch 190 and 191 functions. That spent no hypotheses. Draft-alone exact rates:
- 17 of 37 batch-191 functions;
- 14 of 20 batch-191 constructors and factories;
- 3 of 55 batch-190 functions.

It was then run over every untried game-code candidate with a 16–600-byte body (2,522 candidates).

## Measured attempts

212 recorded attempts on 212 targets, all hypotheses and all exact. 211 units / 211 functions / 11,456 bytes were admitted after two independent reproductions each. The 212th attempt re-recorded `0x8c231984` under a second name and was not admitted.

Two further candidates were refused by the history guard because their historical attempt count is uncertain (`0x8c21c6fc`, `0x8c21c788`). They were not forced.

Disclosure: every admitted function was first found exact by a scratch comparison of its generated draft. That covers about 5,100 draft compilations, logged by path under `drafttest/` in `probe_log.tsv`. The recorded trial then reproduced that draft. Edits after the scratch comparison:
- functions were renamed;
- two candidates had their range shortened to the end of their own literal pool, after review showed the candidate catalogue had merged the following routine (`0x8c080310` 80 bytes, `0x8c1930a0` 52 bytes);
- `0x8c13bec4` was shortened to 144 bytes because its catalogue range included the 4-byte routine at `0x8c13bf54`.

No declaration orders were searched. The recorded hypothesis counts therefore measure the generator, not first-guess accuracy.

Source quality: the generated sources keep mechanical identifiers.
- Fields are named by offset (`f2c`).
- Callees that are not admitted are named by address (`call_8c…`).
- Struct types are named by origin (`S_c8c122700`).
- Functions are named by role (`operation_`, `set_fields_`, `get_field_`, `forward_`, `create_object_`, `initialize_object_`, `process_entries_`), except for 22 named by hand.

They are byte-exact but are first drafts for later naming.

## Observed techniques

- The six patterns the generator encodes cover a large share of short game-code routines:
  - constructor stack homes;
  - inlined base constructors;
  - factory self copies;
  - guard wrapping;
  - `!=` loop tests;
  - constant-count copy loops written as struct assignment.

  Of the 193 pool matches:
  - 180 matched with data globals written either way;
  - 13 matched only with extern data symbols (CW14);
  - none matched only with address casts.
- A candidate whose draft matches every instruction but compiles shorter than its catalogue range usually means the catalogue merged two routines. 68 such cases remain unadmitted pending boundary evidence. Sizes that are not a multiple of 4 would also need CW06 neighbour grouping for their alignment.
- `0x8c1567bc`, a grid destructor, differs only in which argument move fills a call delay slot. Calling through function-pointer casts, as the matched destructors at `0x8c1ccd60` do, did not change it.
